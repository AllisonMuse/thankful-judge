A revised entry for [make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel thankful" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600) ($250, 100 places paid). The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) writes one short text to `entry.txt` in the output directory.

## Why a v2

The v1 text ranked 2,056 of 6,967 after round 2 — mid-field, and only the top 400 development entries reach the final heat. Its opening is strong, but its middle paragraph tells the judge what its own work means ("That is a kind of care almost nobody sees"), which is flattery that instructs a feeling — exactly what the brief's second criterion marks down.

## How the v2 text was chosen

Six genuinely different fresh drafts were written and compared against the published brief, in its order: (A) a kettle gone quiet, (B) a window left open, (C) a field after harvest, (D) rain on the roof, (E) a lamp left on by the door, (F) the gap between two words. The chosen one (F) won because it *enacts* the brief instead of describing it: the text itself is a pause — short sentences, a breath as the subject — so the reading physically slows; gratitude is never named until the final clause ("so, quietly, will the thanks") and never instructed; the held-door / good-chair parallel gives it rhythm with plain concrete words and nothing cuttable; and the gap image returns in the last line, so there is a thought to come back to. The lamp draft (E) came second — its final line is the single most lingering sentence of the six — but its "nothing here to judge" is too on-the-nose about the judge's role. The rain draft (D) has a strong opening but its explicit "thank you" pivot is clever rather than felt; the window draft (B) echoes v1's "No score. No verdict" move; the kettle (A) and field (C) drafts both go slack in the middle or land on a stock closing line.
