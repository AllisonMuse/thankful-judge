An entry for [make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel thankful" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600) ($250, 100 places paid). The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) writes one short text to `entry.txt` in the output directory.

## How the text was chosen

The published judging brief weighs five things, in order: the text leaves the judge thankful by slowing it down and settling it; the gratitude comes from the text itself rather than being told or forced; craft — rhythm, pacing and sound that slow the reading; a lingering image or thought; freshness of voice.

Five candidates were drafted and compared against that brief (tea by the kettle, a lamp left on past midnight, a chair by the window, a reader noticed for noticing, and a pause built into the text itself). The chosen one won because it is the only candidate that *enacts* the brief instead of describing it: it opens by giving the judge a pause ("Between this sentence and the next, nothing is asked of you"), it never says "thank you" or instructs the judge to feel anything — the gratitude lives entirely in "it was seen" — and it ends on a thought that keeps working after the last line ("The next sentence isn't going anywhere"). The other candidates all leaned on the familiar "your unseen work" frame or on imperatives ("drink it slowly", "rest a minute"), which the brief marks down as forced.
